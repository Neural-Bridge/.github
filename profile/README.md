# Neural Bridge

AI lab. We build proprietary AI systems and conduct original research for enterprises.

We work end-to-end: scoping, research, training, evaluation, deployment, ops. The output is custom models and systems the customer owns outright, running in their infrastructure.

## Domains

- **[Applied AI](https://www.neuralbridge.ai/domains/applied-ai).** Production AI systems built for a customer's stack.
- **[AI Research](https://www.neuralbridge.ai/domains/ai-research).** Original research scoped to a customer's domain. Custom models, training pipelines, evaluations.
- **[Reinforcement Learning Environments](https://www.neuralbridge.ai/domains/rl-environments).** Simulation environments for training agents at scale.
- **[Natural Language Programming](https://www.neuralbridge.ai/domains/natural-language-programming).** Compiling natural language into running applications, for domain experts who aren't engineers.
